Rules the form checks

  • The list of scorers is limited to players who have goals left in the totals.
  • Up to 3 passes per goal, all from the scoring team, none of them the scorer (4 people).
  • Own goal: its author is from the other team; up to 4 involved players from the scoring team (5 people).
  • Each pass in the totals belongs to exactly one goal.
  • A header moves the score by 2, an own goal counts for the opponent.
  • A penalty has no passes (the form warns if you add one).
  • Publishing needs all goals placed and all passes used, or no order at all.
